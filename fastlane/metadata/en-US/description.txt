One more coffee? Yes. Here is until when.

Cutoff is the caffeine calculator that says yes. Tell it when you go to bed and what you usually drink, and it shows one line: "One more coffee? Yes, until 2:40 PM", with a countdown of the room you have left. Every cup you log moves the time.

It is an estimate, not a promise: Cutoff uses a published caffeine model and average half-life, and you can tune both.

HOW IT WORKS
Each drink is absorbed over about an hour, then cleared at caffeine's half-life. Cutoff adds up every drink from the last two days and finds the latest minute your usual drink could still clear down to your sleep line by bedtime. The research behind it is listed in the app.
