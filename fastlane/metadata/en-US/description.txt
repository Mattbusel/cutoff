This app tells you exactly when to stop drinking caffeine if you want to sleep tonight.

Tell Cutoff when you go to bed and what you usually drink. It shows one number: "Latest coffee: 2:34 PM", with a countdown, and it moves every time you log a cup.

HOW IT WORKS
Each drink is absorbed over about an hour, then cleared at caffeine's half-life. Cutoff adds up every drink from the last two days and finds the latest minute your usual drink could still clear down to your sleep line by bedtime. The research behind it is listed in the app.
